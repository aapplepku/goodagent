# Amazon Berkeley Objects sample

Product listing text and thumbnails: Amazon.com, Creative Commons Attribution 4.0
International. Source dataset and archives: Matthieu Guillaumin, Thomas Dideriksen,
Kenan Deng, Himanshu Arora, Jasmine Collins, and Jitendra Malik.

Source: https://amazon-berkeley-objects.s3.us-east-1.amazonaws.com/index.html
License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Open Commerce selected 48 listings and mirrored their thumbnails without image
modification. Prices, stock, popularity, ratings and engagement are synthetic demo
values, not live Amazon data. Full provenance, image source URLs and SHA-256 digests:
examples/datasets/amazon-berkeley-objects in the source repository.
