# Code of Conduct

Participants should be respectful, constructive and considerate of differences
in experience, background and access needs. Critique code and ideas, not people.
Disagreement is welcome; personal attacks, harassment, threats, discriminatory
behavior, unwanted sexual attention and disclosure of another person's private
information are not.

Use synthetic reproductions. Do not share customer records, secrets or private
security details in public discussions. Respect contributors' time and boundaries;
do not pressure anyone to disclose personal information or work without agreement.

The repository owner is responsible for enforcing these expectations in project
issues, reviews and other project-managed spaces. Depending on severity and
context, responses may include a private warning, content removal, a temporary
participation restriction or removal from project spaces. Reports should be handled
with discretion, and retaliation against a good-faith report is unacceptable.

Report concerns privately through an existing trusted contact with the owner.
A public reporting contact must be selected and verified before community launch;
none is invented here. The project cannot promise anonymity or a response SLA
before that process is established.
